## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-end enthusiast PC hardware and component retail: precision-machined, technically uncompromising, and surgically clean. It addresses serious builders, creative professionals, and high-performance gamers who view hardware as precision tools and artisanal engineering rather than toys.

The visual direction combines high-density architectural minimalism with industrial tactility:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep obsidian baselines, milled metallic surfaces, and subtle, structural slate framing.
- **Tone:** Authoritative, measured, and ultra-focused.
- **Differentiation:** Rejects juvenile multi-color RGB clutter, aggressive angled polygon cutouts, and neon overload in favor of matte micro-textures, crisp hair-line boundaries, mechanical monospace callouts, and decisive crimson performance accents.

## Colors

The palette establishes a controlled nocturnal environment built on calibrated cool charcoal gradations, letting pure white typography and restrained crimson telemetry guide attention.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas & Base:** Default background sits at `#0f1115` with secondary structural surfaces anchored at `#161920`.
- **Card & Layer Surfaces:** Interactive and grouping containers utilize elevated charcoal `#1c212b`, shifting to `#222834` on active surfaces and hover transitions.
- **Structural Lines:** Structural division utilizes `#2e3748` for crisp, low-diffuse boundaries.
- **Accents:**
  - **Primary Crimson (`#e11d48`):** Reserved exclusively for core transactional triggers, vital benchmark indicators, and key buy states.
  - **Secondary Scarlet (`#ef4444`):** Employed for immediate system notices, stock alerts, and promotional tags.
  - **Tertiary Cyan (`#38bdf8`):** Restrained to technical status telemetry (e.g., clock speeds, PCIe lane metrics, thermal states).
- **Text & Content Hierarchy:**
  - **Primary Text:** `#f8fafc` for maximum legibility on dark layers.
  - **Secondary Text:** `#94a3b8` for specs, technical annotations, and inactive controls.
  - **Muted/De-emphasized:** `#64748b` for subtle system metadata and disabled labels.

## Typography

The typographic system fuses the geometric legibility of Plus Jakarta Sans for UI narratives and commerce flow with the clinical, machine-tooled precision of JetBrains Mono for technical specifications, SKUs, and architecture stats.

### Typographic Governance
- **Technical Specs & Telemetry:** Clock frequencies (e.g., `5.7 GHz`), power draws (`320W TDP`), component dimensions, and interface ports are set in `JetBrains Mono` (`tech-code-*` or `label-sm`).
- **Vietnamese Localization & Diacritics:** Plus Jakarta Sans natively provides balanced diacritic clearance. Maintain an explicit 1.5x minimum line-height multiplier on localized Vietnamese running text to eliminate diacritic collision across multi-line technical descriptions.
- **Numeric Tables & Pricing:** Currency and numerical spec charts should leverage tabular numeric rendering (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to maintain vertical parity during side-by-side hardware comparisons.

## Layout & Spacing

A strict 8pt base grid dictates spacing across all surfaces, ensuring mathematical alignment and dense, scannable data layouts.

### Screen Adaptations & Layout Hierarchy
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column grid system with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and a max canvas width of 1440px. Margin remains at 48px (`3rem`). Complex hardware comparison matrices utilize the full 12 columns with locked left-column parameter indexing.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column grid with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 32px (`2rem`) outer boundaries. Hardware spec tables switch to horizontal paning or multi-card stacking.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 16px (`1rem`) exterior margins. Product cards transition into single-column or tight two-column micro-grids. Sticky bottom purchase controls are mandatory for high-ticket SKUs.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through structural surface stacking and crisp slate borders, rather than soft drop shadows or diffused back-lit colors.

### Elevation Architecture
- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** Flat `#0f1115`. Hosts global navigation framing and baseline layout foundations.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Module Foundation):** `#1c212b` bordered by a 1px solid `#2e3748` outline. No exterior drop shadow; boundaries are clearly defined by edge value contrast.
- **Layer 2 (Elevated & Hover State):** On hover or focus, card background shifts to `#222834` while the border shifts to a focused slate `#475569`. A subtle tinted ambient shadow is introduced: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals, Flyouts & Drawer Navigation):** Surface `#222834` with an explicit 1px `#475569` border and deep directional shadow: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.70)`. Underlaid with an ultra-dark frosted scrim (`rgba(15, 17, 21, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`).
- **Interactive State Cues:** High-performance hardware cards do not glow with RGB gradients. When active, interactive components introduce an interior 1px crimson hairline edge indicator on the lead structural axis.

## Shapes

The geometric vernacular uses restrained corner radiuses (`roundedness: 1`), evoking machined aluminum chassis, heatsink fins, and precision die cuts.

### Corner Rules
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges):** 4px (`0.25rem`). Maintains sharp, industrial precision without being completely razor-edged.
- **Cards & Hardware Module Containers (`rounded-lg`):** 8px (`0.5rem`). Applied to main product units, comparison cells, and spec panels.
- **Modals & Overlays (`rounded-xl`):** 12px (`0.75rem`). Softens high-level dialogs without compromising the mechanical visual tone.
- **Strictly Prohibited:** Full pill radiuses, circular badges, or organic asymmetrical corner radii.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Controls
- **Primary Action (Add to Cart / Pre-order):** Solid `#e11d48` background, `#f8fafc` text, 4px border radius. On hover: shifts to `#ef4444`. On active/pressed: transforms slightly down (`translateY(1px)`) with background darkening to `#be123c`.
- **Secondary Action (Configure / Spec Sheet):** Elevated background `#1c212b` with a 1px solid border `#2e3748` and `#f8fafc` text. Hover shifts background to `#222834` and border to `#475569`.
- **Ghost / Utility (Wishlist / Quick Compare):** Transparent background, 1px border of `#2e3748`, text `#94a3b8`. Hover transitions text to `#f8fafc` and border to `#94a3b8`.

### Product Cards
- **Base Card:** Milled surface `#1c212b` wrapped in a 1px `#2e3748` border. Padding is strictly 16px (`1rem`).
- **Media Slot:** Top section features a `#161920` inset viewport with a 4px inner radius to showcase hardware photography against a neutral gradient.
- **Spec Telemetry Strip:** Directly beneath the product title, display a horizontal row of high-priority specs (e.g., `24GB GDDR6X`, `VRAM: 384-bit`) using `JetBrains Mono` at `tech-code-sm` with a subdued `#94a3b8` color.
- **Hover Behavior:** 150ms ease-out elevation shift to `#222834`, with border transitioning to `#475569`. The product image subtly scales (`scale(1.02)`), with no RGB neon glows.

### Technical Spec Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips (In Stock / Pre-order):** Background `#161920` with a 1px border. "In Stock" uses a 6px circular dot indicator in `#10b981`. "Low Stock" utilizes `#ef4444`. Text uses `JetBrains Mono` 11px uppercase.
- **Hardware Architecture Tags:** `#1c212b` background, 1px `#2e3748` border, `#94a3b8` typography. No filled vibrant pills.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Container:** Dark charcoal `#161920` background with 1px `#2e3748` border and 4px radius. 
- **Typography:** Value input in `#f8fafc` (Plus Jakarta Sans, 14px); placeholder in `#64748b`.
- **Focus State:** 1px stroke changes to `#e11d48` accompanied by a flat crimson focus ring: `0 0 0 1px #e11d48`.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Radios
- **Checkbox:** 16x16px square, 2px corner radius, `#161920` background, 1px `#2e3748` stroke. Checked state: Solid `#e11d48` with a white checkmark icon.
- **Segmented Radio (RAM/Storage Selectors):** Contiguous modular block of `#1c212b`. Selected option features a `#222834` fill, 1px `#e11d48` border, and bold `#f8fafc` label; unselected options sit at `#94a3b8` with subtle vertical slate dividers.

### Technical Data Tables (Spec Comparison Engine)
- **Header:** `#161920` fill, `#94a3b8` text in `tech-code-sm`, uppercase with subtle letter spacing.
- **Rows:** Alternating horizontal hairline borders using `#2e3748`. Metric values aligned using `JetBrains Mono` tabular figures. Hovering a table row highlights the entire horizontal slice with a `#222834` overlay.
